Lab groups

A lab group shares tables, templates and favourites among its members.

  • New Lab Group creates a group; Allow members to invite others lets every member invite.
  • Invite Member sends an invitation by email address, with an optional message.
  • Invitations to you appear at the top, to Accept or Reject.
  • View members lists the members; those who manage the group can remove members and cancel invitations.
  • Leave group removes you from a group you did not create, and Delete group removes a group you manage.
