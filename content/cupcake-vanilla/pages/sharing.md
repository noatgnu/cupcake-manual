Title: Sharing a table
Order: 05
Summary: Lab groups, share links, and locked and published tables.
Screens: shared/:token

A table assigned to a lab group is open to the group's members. To show a table to someone without an account, open
it and choose **Share**, then **Generate Share Link**: anyone with the link can view the table without signing in.

- **Copy link** copies the link.
- **Regenerate** replaces the link; the old one stops working.
- **Revoke** removes the link.

A **locked** table cannot be changed, and a **published** table is marked as final rather than a draft.
