Summary: Signing in, creating an account and finding your way around Cupcake Vanilla.
Screens: login, register, metadata

Cupcake Vanilla keeps the sample metadata of proteomics experiments in tables that follow the SDRF format, and
exports them as SDRF or Excel files.

## Signing in

Sign in with your username and password, or with **Login with ORCID** when the site allows it. Tick **Keep me logged
in on this device** to stay signed in.

![](shot:gui-vanilla/vanilla-login)

New users choose **Create Account** when the site allows public registration; otherwise an administrator creates the
account.

## Metadata Management

After signing in, Cupcake Vanilla opens on Metadata Management:

- **Metadata Tables** lists your tables and creates new ones; see [Metadata tables]({filename}metadata-tables.md).
- **Metadata Templates** keeps reusable column and table templates; see [Templates]({filename}templates.md).
- **Quick Actions** creates a template or a table straight away.

![](shot:gui-vanilla/vanilla-home)

The navigation bar leads to the tables, templates, vocabularies, favourites and lab groups, and the account menu to
your profile, device tokens and, for staff, the administration pages.
