Title: Vocabularies
Order: 07
Summary: The term lists that columns suggest values from, and adding your own.
Screens: vocabularies

A vocabulary is a list of terms that a column suggests values from, such as organisms, diseases or instruments.
**Vocabularies** lists them with who they are available to, their number of terms, their status and when they were
last loaded. Vocabularies provided by a plugin wait for approval by staff before they can be used.

![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-01-list)

## Adding a vocabulary

1. Choose **New vocabulary**, then enter the kind of term, its SDRF short name, a description and a label; the key is
   made from the label.

    ![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-02-new)

2. Create it. It has no terms yet.

    ![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-03-created)

3. Choose **Import terms** and pick an OBO, OWL, CSV or TSV file. For CSV and TSV files, choose which columns hold the
   names, ids, descriptions and synonyms. **Add and update terms** keeps the terms already there; **Replace all
   terms** replaces them.

    ![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-04-import)

4. The vocabulary now has its terms.

    ![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-05-imported)

**Browse terms** searches the terms, edits them, adds new ones and marks obsolete ones.

![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-06-terms)

**Delete** removes a vocabulary that no column uses.

![](shot:steps-vanilla/vocabulary-07-delete)
