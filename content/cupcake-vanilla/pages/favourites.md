Title: Favourites
Order: 08
Summary: Keeping values you use often, for yourself, your lab group or everyone.
Screens: favorites

A favourite is a value kept for a column, offered first when you edit that column's values. A favourite is
**personal**, shared with a **lab group**, or **global**, for everyone.

![](shot:gui-vanilla/vanilla-favorites)

**Favorite Management** searches favourites by name or value, scope, lab group and column type. **Add Favorite** adds
one: the column name, such as `characteristics[organism]`, the value, an optional display name, and its scope. Values
are also added from the value editor with **Add to Favorites**.
