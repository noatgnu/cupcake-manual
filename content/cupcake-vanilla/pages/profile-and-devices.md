## Profile

**User Profile** shows your account and changes your name, password and email address; each change asks for your
current password, and a new email address is confirmed by email.

![](shot:gui-vanilla/vanilla-profile)

**Appearance** chooses the theme: **Default**, **E-Ink** for e-ink displays, or **Stylized Sci-Fi Compact**. Light or
dark mode is switched in the navigation bar.

## Device tokens

A device token lets a device, such as a lab display, use Cupcake Vanilla as you without your password.

![](shot:gui-vanilla/vanilla-devices)

**New Token** creates one with a label, a description, read-only or read and write access, and an optional expiry
date. Copy the token when it is shown: it is not shown again. **Rotate token** replaces a token, and **Delete**
removes it.
