Title: Transcription models
Order: 10
Summary: The speech-to-text models kept for every instance.
Screens: dashboard:models

Models, in the top menu (staff only), lists the transcription models kept on the cluster for every instance, so they
are downloaded once. One of them is the default that new instances use.

![](shot:screens/06-models)

**Pull** downloads a model. **Make this the default model** changes the default; an instance whose owner chose a model
keeps that choice. **Download again** repairs a model, and **Remove from the cache** deletes it.
