Title: Logs
Order: 11
Summary: Reading the output of cupcake-master's and each instance's programs.
Screens: dashboard:logs, dashboard:instance_logs

Logs, in the top menu (staff only), shows the recent output of cupcake-master's own programs. Each instance's logs are
under the **Logs** button on its page, for its owner and staff.

![](shot:screens/08-logs)

Choose the program, how many lines to show, and whether to follow new lines; **Download** saves them.
