Title: Projects
Order: 02
Summary: Grouping jobs and sessions into projects.
Screens: home/projects

A project groups the instrument jobs and sessions of one piece of work. **My Projects** lists your projects;
selecting one shows its information, jobs and sessions.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-projects)

**Create Project** creates one with a name and an optional description, and **Edit Project** changes them. A job's
project can also be created while submitting the job; see [Instrument jobs]({filename}jobs.md).
