## Site settings

**Site Configuration** sets the site's name, logo and colour, registration and ORCID sign-in, the reagent booking
deletion window, and the default Whisper model for transcription. **UI Feature Visibility** shows or hides metadata
tables, instruments, sessions, protocols, messages, notifications, storage, live sessions and billing.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-admin-site)

## Background workers

**Worker Status** shows the background workers, the job each is running, and the queues. Only superusers see it.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-admin-workers)

## Users

**User Management** lists accounts and creates and edits them, with their active, staff and superuser roles.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-users)
