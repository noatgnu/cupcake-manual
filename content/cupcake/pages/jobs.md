Title: Instrument jobs
Order: 09
Summary: Submitting samples to be run on an instrument, with their metadata, and following the job.
Screens: jobs, jobs/submit, jobs/:id

An instrument job asks the staff running an instrument to run your samples. Each job has a metadata table describing
its samples.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-jobs)

## Submitting a job

**Submit New Job** walks through the submission:

1. **Job & Project**: name the job, and choose a project or create one.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-01-title)

2. **Lab Group & Staff**: choose the lab group and the staff who will run the job, and enter the funder and cost
   centre.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-02-lab-group)

3. **Samples**: enter the number of samples, which is the number of rows in the metadata table.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-03-samples)

4. **Template**: choose the metadata table template for the samples.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-04-template)

5. **Review & Submit**: create the metadata table, fill in its columns, and submit.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-05-review)

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-06-ready)

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-07-submitted)

Until it is submitted, a job is a draft: **Continue Editing Draft** returns to it.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-job-submit)

## After submitting

Staff review the job and fill in the columns only they edit, such as the instrument settings.

![](shot:steps-cupcake/job-08-staff-columns)

**Instrument Jobs** lists jobs by status (draft, submitted, pending, in progress, completed, cancelled) and type. A
job's page shows its information, assigned staff, metadata table and timeline, and its annotations: notes, files,
recordings and calculations, from you and from staff.
