## Messages

**Messages** lists your message threads; selecting one shows its messages. Type a message and press Ctrl+Enter to
send it.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-messages)

## Notifications

**Notifications** lists what Cupcake has told you, such as low stock, maintenance due and booking changes, by type,
priority and whether you have read them. **Mark All as Read** marks them all.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-notifications)
