A session is one run of one or more protocols. Everyone in the session sees the same steps, notes and timers.

## Starting a session

1. On a protocol, choose **Start Session**, name the session and choose **Create & Start Session**. A public session
   can be viewed by everyone.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/protocol-07-new-session)

2. **Sessions** lists your sessions; **Open Session** opens one.

    ![](shot:steps-cupcake/protocol-08-sessions)

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-sessions)

## Working through the steps

The session shows the protocol's sections and the current step, with its instructions and the reagents it needs.
**Previous Step** and **Next Step** move between steps, in a single view or as a list.

![](shot:steps-cupcake/protocol-09-session)

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-session)

## Annotations

**Add Annotation** adds to the step:

- **Text**: a note.
- **Upload**: an image, video, audio recording or file.
- **Record**: an audio, video or screen recording; audio and video can be transcribed automatically.
- **Sketch**: a drawing.
- **Book**: a booking of an instrument for the step, showing the instrument's other bookings.
- **Calc** and **Molarity**: a calculator and a molarity calculator, kept with their history.

![](shot:steps-cupcake/protocol-10-note)

![](shot:steps-cupcake/protocol-11-noted)

**Book** beside a reagent reserves a quantity of it from storage. A booking can be deleted until the site's deletion
window before it starts.

## Timers

A step with a duration has one timer, shared by everyone in the session: starting, pausing or resetting it does so
for everyone.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-session-timer)

## Who can take part

**Edit** on a session sets its name, its planned dates and its **editors** and **viewers**: editors can change the
session, viewers can only view it. **Live Session** opens a video call with the others in the session.

## Exporting

**Export HTML** saves the session with its protocols and annotations as a web page. **Export to protocols.io** posts
it as protocols.io runs; see [protocols.io]({filename}protocols-io.md).
