Title: Timers
Order: 05
Summary: Timers on their own, and the timers of session steps.
Screens: protocols/timers

**Timers** lists your timers: those of session steps and standalone ones. Selecting one shows its duration, status
and the history of when it was started, paused and reset, and for a session timer, its session and step.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-timers)

**New Timer** creates a standalone timer with a name and a duration in seconds. **Start**, **Pause** and **Reset**
control it, and **Delete Timer** removes it.
