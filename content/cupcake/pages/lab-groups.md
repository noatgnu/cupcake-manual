Title: Lab groups
Order: 12
Summary: Lab groups and sub-groups, their members and what members may do.
Screens: home/lab-groups, lab-groups/invitations/:id/accept

A lab group shares protocols, storage, jobs and metadata among its members. Groups can have sub-groups; open a group
to see its sub-groups and members.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-lab-groups)

- **Create Lab Group** creates a group, and **Create Sub-Group** one inside the open group. **Allow members to invite
  others** and **Allow members to process jobs** apply to every member.
- **Invite Members** invites users; staff can add users directly without an invitation. An invitation is accepted or
  rejected from its link.
- **Permissions** gives each member **View**, **Invite**, **Manage** (change the group's settings and permissions)
  and **Process Jobs** (run the group's jobs).
