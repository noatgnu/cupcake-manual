Title: Storage and reagents
Order: 08
Summary: Storage locations, the reagents in them, their quantities and who can use them.
Screens: storage, storage/:id

**Storage Management** shows storage locations as a tree, such as rooms, freezers, racks and boxes, and the reagents
kept in each.

![](shot:gui-cupcake/cupcake-storage)

## Locations

**Add Storage** adds a location, inside the selected one when one is selected. **Manage Access** chooses the lab groups
that can use a location.

## Reagents

**Add Reagent** adds a reagent to the selected location with its quantity, unit, molecular weight, expiry date and
notes. **Notify on low stock** sends a notification when its quantity falls below the threshold.

Each reagent's actions:

- **Add Quantity** and **Reserve Quantity** record what is added and taken; **Usage Journal** lists every change.
- **Documents** keeps its safety data sheets, certificates and other files.
- **Manage Metadata** describes it with SDRF fields.
- **Copy Share Link** copies a link to it, and its access settings share it with lab groups, users or everyone.

**Search reagents** finds reagents by name, optionally in nested locations and by molecular weight. **Scan Barcode**
finds them with the camera.
