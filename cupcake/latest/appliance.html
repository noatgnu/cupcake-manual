Running the appliance

Only on a Cupcake appliance.

Storage

Storage Management mounts a USB drive by its label, or an NFS or SMB share, at /mnt/cupcake-data.

Backups

Backup Management backs up the database, the uploaded files, or both, to the mounted storage or another folder under /mnt/cupcake-data or /opt/cupcake/backups, and lists the backups made.

Wi-Fi

WiFi Management connects to a network with a password (WPA2 Personal) or an institutional account (WPA2 Enterprise, such as eduroam).

Remote access

Remote Access shows whether the appliance is on a Tailscale tailnet or a Cloudflare tunnel, and the commands that set them up.

Plugins

Plugins lists the installed plugins and their state, starts, stops and restarts them, installs one from a .tar.gz file, and uninstalls them.

Power

Power restarts the backend or the appliance, or shuts it down. After shutting down, wait until its lights stop before unplugging it.
