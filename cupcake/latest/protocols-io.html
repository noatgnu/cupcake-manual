protocols.io

Connecting your account

On your profile, Connected Accounts leads to protocols.io Account: paste an access token from the protocols.io developer page. Replace token changes it and Disconnect removes it from Cupcake.

Importing

Import from protocols.io imports a protocol from its address or DOI. With an account connected, Browse my protocols.io lists the protocols in your workspaces to import from.

Exporting a protocol

Export to protocols.io on a protocol copies its title, description, materials, steps and step times onto a protocol on protocols.io. Create an empty protocol there, in the workspace it belongs in, then choose it or paste its address: its content is replaced. The scaling factor multiplies the quantities of scalable reagents.

Exporting a session

Export to protocols.io on a session posts each of its protocols as a protocols.io run, with the steps' annotations as comments on the matching steps. A protocol whose steps were varied in the session is exported as a new protocol first. Comments show as Anonymous on protocols.io, and uploaded files can be opened by anyone with their address.
