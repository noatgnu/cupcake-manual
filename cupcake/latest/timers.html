Timers

Timers lists your timers: those of session steps and standalone ones. Selecting one shows its duration, status and the history of when it was started, paused and reset, and for a session timer, its session and step.

New Timer creates a standalone timer with a name and a duration in seconds. Start, Pause and Reset control it, and Delete Timer removes it.
